If we want to understand the association between $Y$ and $X$, then we still wish to estimate $f$, but our goal is no t necessarily to make predictions for $Y$. 
Thus, we want to know the exact form of $\hat{f}$, most of the times in order to answer one of the following:
1. Which predictors are associated with the response?
2. What's the relationship between the response and each predictor?
3. Can this relation be summarized with a linear equation, or is the relation more complicated?
